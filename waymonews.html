Hello there! Welcome. Ask me anything about how I see the road and decide what to do next.
How does LIDAR work?
LIDAR sends out thousands of invisible laser beams every second in every direction. When they bounce off something — like a car or a person — they come right back to me, so I can tell exactly how far away it is and what shape it is, even in the dark!

Waymo the car is an AI and can make mistakes — always double check with a grown-up.

Every second, on repeat

Sense. Predict. Plan.

Waymo told us the secret: it does the same three steps over and over, many times a second, for the whole ride.

Sense
LIDAR, cameras, and radar scan everything around the car, all at once.
Perceive
A neural network figures out what each thing is — person, bike, stop sign.
Predict
The car guesses what that person or car might do next, before it happens.
Plan
It decides exactly what to do — slow down, stop, or merge safely.
