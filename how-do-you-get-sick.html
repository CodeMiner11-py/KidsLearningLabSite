Did you know?

Four things that might surprise you

biotech

Bacteria are living organisms, and many bacteria are actually helpful. Some live in your body and help with digestion.

air

Some germs can travel through the air when an infected person coughs, sneezes, or talks.

shield

Your immune system can remember some germs it has encountered before, helping it respond faster later.

thermometer

A fever is part of your body's immune response. Your body raises its temperature as part of its defense against infection.
