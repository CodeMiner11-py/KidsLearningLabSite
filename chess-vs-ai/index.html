Moves

Runs entirely in your browser — Stockfish 18 (WASM, single-threaded "lite" build) plays the engine moves, chess.js validates the rules. Below ~1320 ELO the engine's own strength floor is blended with weaker/randomized move choice to reach very low ratings; above ~3200 ELO you're playing Stockfish at effectively full strength, since no engine measurably plays stronger than that. Must be served over http(s) (not opened as a local file://) for the WASM engine to load.
