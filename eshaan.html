About

A kid who builds things.

Eshaan Buddhisagar is a 13-year-old creator from California, USA who makes technology and science easy to understand for other kids.

He began the Kids Learning Lab podcast at nine and has since released more than 100 episodes on topics like the water cycle, Disney secrets, and cheese.

His passion for technology and space pushes him past content into apps and books. Every episode is kid-made, from writing and editing to recording and publishing.

He is the author of Python Learning Lab, a programming book for kids.
