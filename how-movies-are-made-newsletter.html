Four things that might surprise you

videocam

The very first movie cameras had to be hand-cranked the whole time — and they had no sound at all.

chat_bubble

Since silent films had no sound, actors used big, exaggerated faces — and "title cards" popped up for important lines.

grain

Foley artists make movie sound effects from everyday stuff. Walking on snow? That's just a box of corn starch.

pets

Even in "real" movies, plenty is fake — the dinosaurs in Jurassic Park were never actually there.

Silent
No sound at all. Actors acted big, and title cards showed key words.
Talkies
Sound arrives! Audiences were stunned to hear actors actually speak.
Color
Black-and-white gives way to full color on screen.
Digital 4K
